Where to check government jobs

These are the official websites. Always read the official notification before applying, especially the last date and fee.

SSO Rajasthan: Recruitment Portal

Almost every Rajasthan government job application goes through your SSO ID. Log in, open the Recruitment Portal and complete One-Time Registration (OTR) early.

Official site ↗

RSSB: Rajasthan Staff Selection Board

State-level recruitment, including the Common Eligibility Test (CET), patwari, teacher and junior engineer posts.

Official site ↗

RPSC: Rajasthan Public Service Commission

RAS and other gazetted and senior posts. Official advertisements are on the commission's website.

Official site ↗

Indian Army: Join Indian Army (Agniveer and officer entries)

Jhunjhunu has a long tradition of service in the armed forces. Army recruitment notices and registration are on this official site.

Official site ↗

Sainik School Jhunjhunu: Recruitment tab

The school posts its teaching and non-teaching vacancies, and their application forms, in the Recruitment section of its website.

Official site ↗

National Career Service (Government of India)

A free central-government portal for private and government jobs, job fairs and career counselling.

Official site ↗

Stay safe from job scams

  • Never pay anyone to get a job. Official application fees are paid only through the official portal.
  • Check the web address. Rajasthan government sites end in .rajasthan.gov.in, and central ones in .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • Ignore WhatsApp or Telegram messages promising a 'guaranteed' or 'direct' government job.
  • If you've lost money to a job scam, call 1930 or report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
