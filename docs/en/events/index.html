Annual festivals and fairs

Shekhawati Festival (Shekhawati Utsav)

When
Held every year. 2027 dates not yet announced.
Where
Shekhawati region (Jhunjhunu, Sikar, Churu)

Organised by Rajasthan's Department of Tourism with the district administrations. Past editions have included heritage walks, folk performances, traditional rural games and Rajasthani food. Past dates have varied between February and March.

Sources: utsav.gov.in

Rani Sati Mela

When
Every year on Bhadrapada Amavasya (usually August or September). The 2027 date follows the Hindu calendar.
Where
Rani Sati Temple, Jhunjhunu city

The annual fair at the Rani Sati Dadi temple draws pilgrims from across India and abroad.

Sources: rajras.in
