Advertise with us

Jhunjhunu Blog is building a local audience in Jhunjhunu district. If you want to reach the people of Jhunjhunu, here's how we can work together.

Ways to work with us

Sponsored episode

Support an episode of JHUNJHUNU TALKS. Your brand is credited as sponsor. The guest and the questions stay our choice.

Business spotlight

A clearly labelled feature on your business: its story, its people, what it offers.

Event partnership

Coverage and promotion for your event in Jhunjhunu district.

Social media package

Labelled posts and Reels across our Instagram and Facebook.

Our rules for paid content

  • Every paid piece is labelled 'Sponsored'.
  • We don't publish claims we can't check, such as 'best in Jhunjhunu' or 'guaranteed results'.
  • Paying doesn't buy a positive story, an interview or a 'verified' badge.
  • We may decline work that doesn't fit our editorial standards.

Talk to us

We'll share packages and pricing after understanding what you need. Send us your business name and what you'd like to promote.
