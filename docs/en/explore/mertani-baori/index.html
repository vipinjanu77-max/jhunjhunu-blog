Mertani Baori

Jhunjhunu city

A historic stepwell in Jhunjhunu, with flights of steps descending through arches.

Mertani Baori is one of Jhunjhunu's historic stepwells, built to store the region's most precious resource: water. Flights of steps descend through arches to the water level.

It is a reminder of how towns in this dry region managed water before piped supply.
